1. Understanding Cookies and Local Storage Technologies

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They perform crucial functions such as remembering user interface preferences, enabling seamless navigation between long-form masterclasses, and providing aggregated analytics regarding page performance. Modern web technologies like HTML5 Local Storage are similarly used to preserve your customized lighting themes without repeated server requests.

2. How CandleMealCove Deploys Cookie Technologies

CandleMealCove uses cookies and local storage tokens strictly to improve your reading experience and maintain website stability. For instance, when you select our Candlelit Amber Glow or Twilight Midnight ambiance modes, or scale the font size of a supper recipe, a lightweight token is stored in your local browser so your visual comfort remains consistent throughout your visit. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies to monitor your online behavior across external platforms.

3. Categories of Cookies Used on Our Website

We classify our cookies into two main operational categories. Strictly Necessary & Preference Cookies are vital for remembering your active ambiance theme, font scaling choices, and mobile navigation status; disabling these cookies may cause your visual preferences to reset upon page refresh. Performance & Analytics Cookies (such as Google Analytics) aggregate anonymized statistics regarding article readership, scroll depth, and loading speed, helping our editorial team understand which supper guides provide the greatest practical value to home cooks.

4. Managing and Clearing Cookies via Your Browser

You have full autonomy to inspect, block, or delete cookies at any time through your web browser's privacy settings. Major browsers (including Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge) provide intuitive controls to reject third-party tracking cookies or clear stored cookie caches upon browser exit. Please note that while our culinary guides remain completely readable without cookies, personalized visual themes may reset between visits.

Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated modification date.
