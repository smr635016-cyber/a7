1. Introduction and Scope of Policy

CandleMealCove, accessible at candlemealcove.com and located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data rights of all visitors who engage with our culinary essays, ambiance planners, and hearth guides. This Privacy Policy sets forth our clear protocols regarding the collection, handling, storage, and defense of personal and technical information gathered through your interactions with our website.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

We collect personal identification data only when you voluntarily provide it—such as when submitting a correspondence note via our contact form or requesting evening recipe guidance. This information typically includes your name, email address, and message content. Furthermore, our secure web servers automatically gather non-identifying technical analytics, including browser type, operating system, internet protocol address, referring URLs, and reading duration across our masterclasses.

3. Operational Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

All information collected by CandleMealCove is utilized exclusively to maintain website security, respond to your culinary inquiries, optimize interface usability across mobile and desktop devices, and understand aggregate reading patterns to plan future seasonal guides. We process this data based on our legitimate interest in operating an informative dining publication, as well as your direct consent when submitting communications to our editorial desk.

4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics Telemetry

Our website uses standard HTTP cookies, browser local storage tokens, and Google Analytics mechanisms to assess audience engagement, monitor article reading depth, and preserve your customized interface settings (such as our 3-mode candlelight theme selector). These cookies do not access private files or extract sensitive personal data from your device. You may disable or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time without impacting content accessibility.

5. Non-Monetization and Third-Party Data Sharing

We maintain an uncompromising standard never to sell, rent, monetize, or trade reader personal data with commercial data brokers, advertising exchanges, or third-party marketing companies. Information is shared strictly with essential infrastructure partners (such as certified hosting providers and transactional email systems) who operate under strict confidentiality agreements and process data solely on our behalf.

6. Data Security, Protection, and Retention

We implement comprehensive technical, administrative, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or destructive breach. We retain user correspondence records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of communication or until a reader formally requests the permanent deletion of their correspondence history from our systems.

7. Global Reader Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, CandleMealCove upholds your fundamental rights to review, modify, export, or permanently delete any personal data maintained by our organization. Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) and California enjoy specific rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively, including the right to opt out of data processing and to receive a transparent accounting of all collected data categories without penalty.

8. Hearth Desk Contact Details

If you have questions, concerns, or formal data access requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data protection protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer by mail at CandleMealCove, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by calling our desk at +1-888-777-5845. We are committed to addressing all formal inquiries thoroughly and promptly.
