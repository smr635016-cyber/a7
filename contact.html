Hearth FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best candle type for the dining table?

Always choose 100% natural, unscented beeswax or soy tapers. Scented paraffin candles can clash with culinary aromas and release synthetic soot.

How do I prevent eye fatigue when dining solely by candlelight?

Position candles slightly below or above eye level (around 10–12 inches high or on low votive saucers) and offset from your direct line of sight to eliminate direct retinal glare.
