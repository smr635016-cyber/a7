1. Educational and Gastronomic Nature of Content

The evening supper recipes, seasonal menu plans, botanical elixir formulas, and table ambiance suggestions published on CandleMealCove (candlemealcove.com) are intended exclusively for general educational, culinary, and home hospitality purposes. While our recipes are crafted with whole-food integrity, the material presented on this website does not constitute medical, dietary, or clinical nutritional advice, and should never replace consultation with a qualified medical professional.

2. Open-Flame Candle Safety Precautions

Candlelight dining inherently involves open flames. Readers must exercise rigorous personal safety when lighting candles: never leave burning tapers or votives unattended, ensure candleholders are sturdy, heat-resistant, and placed on level surfaces, keep burning wicks away from drafts, flowing linen tablecloths, dried floral arrangements, and curtains, and keep open flames strictly out of reach of children and household pets. CandleMealCove disclaims all liability for accidental property damage or injuries resulting from improper open-flame candle handling.

3. Allergen Awareness and Dietary Sensitivities

Our recipes incorporate diverse wholesome plant ingredients, whole grains, seeds, nuts, spices, and culinary herbs. Readers with known food allergies or dietary sensitivities must review all recipe ingredients carefully and modify formulations according to their personal health requirements. CandleMealCove assumes no responsibility for adverse allergic reactions or culinary sensitivities arising from the preparation of our recipes.

4. Absence of Medical or Health Treatment Claims

Any references to the calming effects of botanical herbs (such as chamomile, lavender, or holy basil) or the circadian benefits of dim evening lighting reflect traditional culinary practices, sensory wellness traditions, and general lifestyle observations. These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) or any medical authority, and our recipes and elixirs are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any illness or sleep disorder.

5. Recipe Outcomes and Oven Calibration

Cooking and baking times naturally vary based on home oven calibration, altitude, cookware material (such as cast iron vs. ceramic earthenware), and ingredient freshness. Readers are advised to use cooking thermometers and sensory cues (such as aroma, bubbling, and golden crust development) to assess doneness accurately.

CandleMealCove maintains strict editorial independence.
